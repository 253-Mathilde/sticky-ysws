# your digital drawing and sticker site

https://253-mathilde.github.io/sticky-ysws/


On this site you can move around stickers and draw.
It has a moving name and an icon at the top.
It also has a custom highlighter color.
And ofc a custom cursor.

<img width="1916" height="914" alt="Screenshot 2026-09-24 135542" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/becbcab2-0a21-4270-bf70-801279278198" />
To run this site, just click the link at the top. To drag a sticker, just click on it and then drag it (while holding the "click").
To draw, just click somewhere random and drag. It is important to not start on a sticker (you can still draw over a sticker tho, but like your start position shouldn't be a sticker).
You can change the width of your brush in the Line brush element (in the menu on the left) and you can also pick a color. Just click on the color and a separate menu will open.
